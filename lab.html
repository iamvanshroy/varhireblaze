About VB Lab

VB Lab is the creative side of VarHireBlaze: 15 small interactive experiences built from scratch to explore what a web browser can do. Some are toys, some are tools and some are games. All of them run on your device with no login and no download.

What you can learn here

  • Pixel Studio and Game Studio show how games are built from grids, tiles and simple physics. Make a level and share it as a link.
  • Sound Machine uses the Web Audio API to synthesise drums and melodies live, the same way our game sounds are made.
  • Physics Playground runs a small custom physics engine with gravity, bounces and collisions.
  • Reality Glitch recreates well-known optical illusions from vision science and shows how each one tricks your brain.
  • Dead Internet Museum is a playful tour of how web design looked in the late 90s and 2000s.

Your data

Pets, aquariums, drawings, beats, cards and secrets are saved only in your browser. Daily Survival and Brain leaderboards store only a nickname you choose and your score. The Game Master sends only a game name, your best score and a mood to write a challenge. Privacy policy.
