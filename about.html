About VarHireBlaze

Free tools to help you get your resume past the filter.

VarHireBlaze is a free AI resume checker. Upload your resume as a PDF and, in a few seconds, you get an ATS-style score, the skills you're missing for the job you want, and specific suggestions to fix your resume before you apply.

Why it exists

Many students and freshers send out dozens of applications and hear nothing back. Often the problem isn't their ability. It's a resume that software can't read, or one that doesn't use the words recruiters search for. Most good resume tools are paid or hide results behind a sign-up. VarHireBlaze is built to give that feedback for free, with no login.

What you can do here

  • Check your resume: ATS score, missing skills and fixes, optionally matched against a job description.
  • Download resume templates: clean, ATS-friendly designs.
  • Get wallpapers: desktop and phone wallpapers, free when you share the site.
  • Read the blog: practical guides on ATS, keywords and writing better bullet points.

Your privacy

Your PDF is read in your own browser and is not stored. If you ask for detailed AI feedback, the text is sent once to our AI provider to generate the answer and isn't saved. There are no accounts. Read the full privacy policy.

Who's behind it

VarHireBlaze is designed and built by Vansh Roy, an aspiring ML engineer and entrepreneur who wanted a resume tool he would actually use himself. Feedback and ideas are always welcome on the contact page.

Note: scores are estimates to help you improve your resume. They aren't a guarantee of how any employer's system will rank you.
