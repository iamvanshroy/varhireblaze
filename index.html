    ATS guide

    What makes a resume ATS-friendly?

    Most companies run resumes through an applicant tracking system (ATS) before a person reads them. All our templates follow these rules. Check yours against them too.

    📄One column

    Tables, text boxes and side columns scramble the text an ATS reads.

    🏷️Standard headings

    Summary, Experience, Education, Skills, Projects. No creative titles.

    🔑Job keywords

    Use the exact skills and tools named in the job description, honestly.

    🖼️No skills in images

    Skill bars, icons and logos are invisible. Write every skill as text.

    ✅Text-based PDF

    If you can't select the words with your mouse, an ATS can't read them.

    📊Results in numbers

    "Cut load time by 40%" beats "worked on performance".

    FAQ

    Questions

    Is it really free?

    Yes. The games, resume check, templates and wallpapers are all free. No login, no card.

    Are the games original?

    Yes. Every game, character, background and sound effect on VarHireBlaze is designed and coded from scratch. Nothing is copied from other games.

    How do the leaderboards work without an account?

    After a run you can type a nickname and post your score. Only the nickname and score are saved. Coins, skins and achievements stay on your own device.

    Do you save my resume?

    No. The PDF is read inside your browser. If you click "AI feedback", only the text is sent to the AI for that one answer and is not stored.

    What is an ATS score?

    Most companies filter resumes with software (an Applicant Tracking System) before a human reads them. The score shows how well your resume is structured and matched for that.

    Are the templates and wallpapers really free?

    Yes. Tap Download and the file is created in your browser and saved to your device. There's nothing to unlock, no sign-up and no payment. If you like them, sharing VarHireBlaze with a friend is always appreciated but never required.
