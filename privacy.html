VarHireBlaze — Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: November 2026

Your resume

Your PDF is read inside your own browser. We do not upload or store your file. If you click "Get detailed AI feedback", the resume text (and job description, if you added one) is sent once to our AI provider (Groq) to generate the answer. We do not save it.

Cookies

We use cookies for basic analytics and, where shown, ads (Google AdSense).

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info.

Share to unlock

Share buttons open WhatsApp, Instagram, X or SMS on your device. We never see who you share with. Unlocked items are remembered only in your own browser.

No accounts

There is no login or sign-up. We don't collect your name or email unless you email us.
