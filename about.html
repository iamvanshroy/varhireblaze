About VarHireBlaze

VB for short. A games-first corner of the internet for people who are job hunting.

VarHireBlaze is a free site where you can play, create and unwind while you look for your next job, and fix your resume when you're ready. Everything runs in your browser. There's no login, no download and no paywall.

What's on VB

  • 31 free games. These include the 8-game Office Life series (Home Office Hustle, Boss Alert, Jam Session, Rise & Grind, Wake Up Rush, Metro Rush, Career Ladder and the VB24 Store), the eight-game Neon Series (Apex Highway, Drift Circuit, Pulse Defender, Rope Rally, Gradient Drop, Glitch Horde, Flipside, Photon Dash), the hire-themed games and a 90s arcade. You also get coins, skins, 24 achievements and global top-10 leaderboards.
  • VB Lab, 15 interactive experiences:
    • Create: Pixel Studio, Sound Machine and Game Studio, where you build and share your own platformer level.
    • Play: Pet Universe, Aquarium, Survival Mode (a new daily arena with a global ranking) and the Daily Brain Challenge.
    • Explore: Physics Playground, Reality Glitch (optical illusions), the Dead Internet Museum, the 60-Second Universe and the Secret Internet.
    • Compete & collect: VB World Records, VB Trading Cards and the AI Game Master.
  • AI resume tracker. Get an ATS-style score, missing skills and specific fixes, optionally matched against a job description.
  • Resume templates and wallpapers for your desktop and phone.
  • The blog. Practical guides on ATS, keywords, resume bullets and office life, plus a web gaming section: how browser games work, their history, safety tips, how to make your first HTML5 game and careers in game development.
  • The VarHireBlaze app. Install it on Android, iPhone, iPad or your laptop from the Get the app section. It opens full-screen from your home screen, with no app store needed.
  • Share buttons on every game. Challenge a friend over WhatsApp, X, Instagram or SMS.

Meet Vansh

The character in the Office Life games and on the homepage is Vansh. He's a cartoon version of the site's creator: brown hair, sunglasses and the eternal job-hunter mood. He slacks off when the boss isn't looking, gets stuck in morning traffic, juggles work-from-home chaos and survives the 6 AM treadmill.

Made from scratch, inspired by the classics

Every game, character, sound, illusion, card and wallpaper on VarHireBlaze was built from scratch. The art is drawn with code, the sounds are made live in your browser, and no code, images, music or levels were copied from any other game. The names, characters and job-hunting twists are our own.

Many of the games are our take on well-loved game genres that anyone can make. Credit where it's due:

  • Hire Racer, Apex Highway, Drift Circuit: inspired by arcade lane racers and drift games.
  • Commute Runner, Hire Rush, Photon Dash, Gradient Drop, Flipside: inspired by endless-runner, one-button rhythm runner, slope-rolling and gravity-flip games.
  • Hire Hopper: inspired by one-tap "flap through the gaps" games.
  • Resume Snake, Paddle Pitch, Glass Ceiling, Spam Storm: inspired by the classic snake, paddle-ball, brick-breaker and fixed-shooter arcade genres from the 70s and 80s.
  • Pulse Defender, Glitch Horde, Rope Rally: inspired by arena shooters, wave-survival games and swinging-rope games.
  • Salary 2048: uses the sliding-tile idea from the open-source game 2048 by Gabriele Cirulli. Our version is written from scratch with salary tiles.
  • Skill Match, Catch the Offer, Hire Cricket, Hire Touchdown, Hire Flight, Golf Cart Dash: inspired by memory-card, catching and simple sports and arcade games.
  • Office Life series and VB Lab: original ideas built around Vansh and job-hunting life. Career Ladder is inspired by endless stair-climbing games, and Metro Rush by side-scrolling runners. VB24 Store is our own made-up shop: the products and their VB packaging are drawn from scratch, with Vansh's real photo as the brand badge. Reality Glitch recreates well-known scientific optical illusions, such as the café wall, Müller-Lyer lines and afterimages, with our own drawings. The Dead Internet Museum is a parody of 90s and 2000s web design in general and uses no real website's brand.

We don't use other companies' game names, logos or characters. If you think something here is too close to someone else's work, tell us on the contact page and we'll change or remove it quickly.

Why it exists

Job hunting is stressful. Many students and freshers send dozens of applications and hear nothing back. VB is a place to take a short break that actually feels good, and to get free, honest feedback on your resume when you're ready to apply again.

Your privacy

  • No accounts. Your coins, best scores, pets, aquarium, cards, pixel art, beats and secrets are saved only in your own browser.
  • Leaderboards store only the nickname you choose and your score.
  • The resume tracker reads your PDF in your browser and doesn't store it. If you ask for AI feedback, the text is sent once to our AI provider to write the answer and isn't saved.
  • The AI Game Master sends only the game name, your best score and the mood you pick to our AI provider (Groq) to write a challenge. Nothing personal is sent, and a built-in generator is used when AI is unavailable.
